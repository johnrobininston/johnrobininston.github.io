Real Analysis

  • The real line:
    • Real Numbers (axioms, completeness, order)
    • Supremum & Infimum
    • Extended reals \(\overline{\mathbb{R}}=[-\infty,\infty]\) (topology, arithmetic conventions)
  • Sequences:
    • Sequence Convergence (limits, Cauchy sequences, subsequences)

    • Convergence of Functions (pointwise, uniform)

  • Riemann integration:

Metric Spaces and Topology

  • Metric spaces:
    • Metric Spaces (metrics, examples such as \(d_\infty\) and \(d_1\) on \(C([0,1])\))
    • Cauchy Sequence, Completeness (\(C([0,1])\) is complete under \(d_\infty\) but not under \(d_1\))
    • Epsilon Ball
    • Lipschitz Continuity
  • Topology:
    • Topology (open sets, closed sets, neighborhoods, interior, closure)
    • Point Set Topologies (bases, Hausdorff spaces, continuity, homeomorphisms)
    • Boundary Points
    • Structure of open sets in \(\mathbb{R}\) (countable unions of open intervals)
  • Compactness:
    • Compactness (compact sets are closed in Hausdorff spaces, continuous bijections from compact spaces are homeomorphisms)
    • Cantor set (closed, nowhere dense, uncountable, measure zero)

Measure Theory

  • Measurable spaces:
    • Measure Spaces
      • Algebra, \(\sigma\)-algebra, monotonicity, finite and countable additivity, set generated \(\sigma\)-algebra.
      • Measures, properties of measures (continuity from above and below, null sets, completeness).
    • Generated Sigma Algebra
    • Borel σ-Algebra and Measure
      • Borel sets, Borel Sigma algebra
      • Borel measure
      • Generators of the Borel \(\sigma\)-algebra on \(\mathbb{R}\) (open, closed, half-open intervals and rays)
    • Product σ-Algebra
    • Pi System, Monotone Class Theorem, Dynkin's π-λ Theorem
  • Constructing measures:
    • Outer Measures (measurable sets, extension of a measure to an outer measure)
    • Caratheodory Extension Theorem
      • Extending from pre-measure to full measure.
    • Lebesgue-Stieltjes measures and locally finite Borel measures on \(\mathbb{R}\) (regularity)
  • Lebesgue measure:
    • Exploration - Lebesgue Measure
    • Vitalli Non-Measurability Theorem
      • Impossible to construct measure without \(\sigma\)-algebra
    • Cantor set and Cantor function
    • Lebesgue versus Borel measurable sets (completeness)
  • Measurable functions:
    • Measurable Function
    • Borel and Lebesgue measurable functions, extended-valued functions
    • Simple functions and approximation by simple functions
    • Almost everywhere equivalence

Integration

  • The Lebesgue integral:
    • Lebesgue Integral (simple functions, nonnegative functions, \(L^1\))
    • Relationship with the Riemann integral (Riemann Integral)
  • Convergence theorems:
    • Monotone Convergence Theorem
    • Fatou Lemma
    • Dominated Convergence Theorem
    • Uniform Integrability (later)
  • Modes of convergence:
    • Pointwise, uniform and almost everywhere (Convergence of Functions, Convergence Almost Surely)
    • Convergence in measure (metrizable on finite measure spaces) (Convergence in Probability)
    • Egoroff’s theorem and Lusin’s theorem
  • Inequalities:
    • Markov Inequality, Chebyshev Inequality
    • Jensen Inequality
    • Hölder Inequality, Minkowski Inequality, Cauchy-Schwartz Inequality
    • Triangle Inequality
  • Product measures:
    • Product measures, Tonelli’s and Fubini’s theorems
    • Counterexamples: completeness of product spaces, non-\(\sigma\)-finite spaces
  • Differentiation of measures (later):
    • Radon-Nikodym Derivative
    • Signed measures
